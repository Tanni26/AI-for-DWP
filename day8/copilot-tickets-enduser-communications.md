## For the Paralegal — "I don't have access to that content"

**What's happening**

Copilot can only read files that you already have permission to access in SharePoint — it works exactly like your own login. If a file or folder has not been shared with you, Copilot will get the same "no access" result that you would get if you tried to open it yourself.

In this case it looks like the NDA folder has not been shared with your account. Hearing about a file in a meeting doesn't automatically give you access to it in SharePoint.

**What to do next**

1. Find out who owns the folder or document (the person who mentioned it in the meeting is a good starting point).
2. Ask them to share it with you in SharePoint — they can right-click the file or folder and choose "Share."
3. Once they have shared it with you, try asking Copilot to summarise it again — it should work straight away.

If you are not sure who to ask, reply to this message and we will help you find the right person.

## For the New Associate — Copilot can't find my case emails

**What's happening**

When a brand-new Microsoft 365 account is created, it takes a little time — usually between 24 and 72 hours — for the system to index your mailbox so Copilot can search through it. During that window, Copilot simply cannot find your emails yet, even though they are sitting right there in Outlook. This is completely normal and will resolve on its own.

**What to do next**

1. If your account was created within the last three days, please wait until Thursday and try again.
2. In the meantime, you can still read and search your emails directly in Outlook — just use the search bar at the top of Outlook as normal.
3. If it has been more than 72 hours since your account was set up and Copilot still cannot find your emails, please reply to this message and we will investigate further.

Welcome to FinBridge — we will get this sorted for you quickly.

## For the Contract Specialist — Copilot gives vague answers about our templates

**What's happening**

Copilot works best when you point it at a specific document rather than asking it a general question. If you ask something like *"What does our contract say about termination?"* without mentioning a specific file, Copilot may answer from general knowledge rather than actually reading your templates. The result sounds plausible but isn't specific to your documents — which is exactly what you're experiencing.

There may also be a short delay before Copilot can read newly added files in SharePoint (it needs to index them first, which can take up to 72 hours). We are checking whether your templates library has been fully indexed.

**What to do next**

Try rephrasing your prompts to reference the document more directly. For example:

- Instead of: *"What does our contract say about termination?"*
- Try: *"Summarise the termination clause in the standard services agreement in our contract templates library."*

- Instead of: *"What are our payment terms?"*
- Try: *"Open the standard supplier contract template and tell me what it says about payment terms and late fees."*

Being specific about the file or folder makes a big difference. If you try this and still get generic answers, please reply to this message with an example prompt and we will investigate whether there is an indexing issue with the library.
