# Leadership Update – FinBridge Win 11 Migration
**Date:** 12 August 2026
**Prepared by:** IT Operations

---

The Windows 11 migration across the FinBridge team has broadly landed well. The majority of staff are working normally in the new environment, and early feedback includes positive remarks about the smoother rollout experience, improved interface options such as dark mode, and general satisfaction with how the programme was managed. For most people, day-to-day work has continued without disruption, which reflects well on the preparation and communications effort from the team.

However, a small number of serious issues remain unresolved and are directly preventing some staff from doing their jobs. The most urgent of these is a complete loss of access to the shared credentials vault, which has been affecting an entire team for at least three days and has already been escalated internally. In parallel, a growing number of engineers are being locked out of the admin console — this started as an isolated case but has since spread, suggesting a wider configuration problem that needs prompt attention. A third group is unable to reach their test environments at all. None of these are cosmetic — they represent work stopped entirely. Both issues are under active investigation and the IT Operations team is treating them as top priorities. We will provide a further update by end of day today.
