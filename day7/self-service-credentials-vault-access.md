# Self-Service Guide: Cannot Access the Credentials Vault

**Try these steps first:**

1. Sign out of the vault completely, close your browser, then sign back in using your updated Windows 11 account credentials (the same username and password you now use to log into your laptop).

2. If that does not work, clear your browser's saved passwords and cache, then try signing in again.

---

**Still blocked? Contact the Service Desk.**

Call or raise a ticket and include these details for a faster fix:

- Your full name and team
- The exact error message or what you see on screen (a screenshot helps)
- How long you have been unable to access the vault
- Whether colleagues in your team are affected too

The Service Desk will escalate this as a priority issue.
