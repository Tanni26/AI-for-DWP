1. If you cannot sign in or your account says it is locked:
- Try signing out fully, close the app, then sign in again.
- If that does not work, wait 10 minutes and try once more before retrying.
- Contact the service desk if you still cannot get in after that. Share your username, the time it started, and the exact message you see so they can reset it quickly.

2. If your files seem to be missing in OneDrive:
- Check the OneDrive cloud icon and make sure it is not showing an error or pause message.
- Open OneDrive online and look for the missing file there.
- Contact the service desk if the file is still missing or you need it urgently. Share the file name, where you last saw it, and whether it is missing on the desktop, in OneDrive, or both.

3. If you cannot get to a shared drive:
- Try closing and reopening File Explorer, then click the drive again.
- If that does not work, sign out and sign back in once.
- Contact the service desk if the drive is still not opening. Share the drive letter or name, what message you get, and whether this is stopping your work so they can fix it faster.
