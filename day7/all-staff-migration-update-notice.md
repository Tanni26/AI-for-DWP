# End User Update – Windows 11 Migration

**Title:** Your Windows 11 Migration – What You Need to Know Today

---

**Notification Message:**

The move to Windows 11 is progressing well and most staff are up and running without issues. A small number of people are experiencing difficulties accessing the credentials vault and admin tools — we know about these and our support teams are actively working to fix them. If you are affected, please try signing out and back in with your new Windows 11 login details; if that does not help, contact the Service Desk and they will prioritise your request. We will keep you updated as things are resolved — thank you for your patience and for bearing with us.
