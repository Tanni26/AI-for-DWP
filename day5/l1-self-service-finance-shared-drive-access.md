# Finance Shared Drive Missing After Sign-In — Self-Help Guide

**Version:** v 1.0 | **Date:** 07/08/2026 | **Status:** Draft

---

## Your files are safe

If your shared drive (S:) is not showing up after you sign in, please don't worry — your files are stored on a central system and are not lost. This is a sign-in timing issue and your data is completely safe.

---

## What may have happened

Sometimes the connection to your shared drive is not ready by the time your computer finishes starting up. Signing out and back in gives it another chance to connect properly.

---

## Steps to try yourself

Work through these steps in order and check after each one whether the drive has come back.

**Step 1 — Sign out and sign back in**
1. Save any open work.
2. Sign out of your computer (click the Start button, then your name, then Sign out).
3. Wait **60 seconds**.
4. Sign back in and wait **2–3 minutes** before checking.
5. Open File Explorer and look for drive **S:** on the left-hand side.

**Step 2 — Restart your computer**
If S: is still missing after Step 1:
1. Save any open work.
2. Restart your computer (Start → Power → Restart).
3. After sign-in, wait **2–3 minutes** before checking File Explorer again.

---

## What not to do

- Do not try to change any settings on your computer.
- Do not contact your manager to unlock access — this is handled by the service desk.
- Do not try more than one restart. If it is still missing, contact the service desk.

---

## Contact the Service Desk if…

Contact the Service Desk if the S: drive is still missing after you have:
- Signed out and back in once, **and**
- Restarted your computer once.

Do not wait and hope it fixes itself — log a ticket so the team can restore your access quickly.

---

## What to tell the Service Desk

Please have the following ready when you call or raise a ticket — it helps the team fix things faster:

- Your full name and work username
- Your computer name (shown at the bottom of the sign-in screen, e.g. DESKTOP-FB041)
- The time you first noticed the drive was missing
- Whether colleagues in the Finance team are seeing the same issue
- Which steps above you have already tried
