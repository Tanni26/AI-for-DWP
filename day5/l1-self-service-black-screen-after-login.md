# Help Guide: Black Screen After Sign-In

Version Header: v 1.0, 07/08/2026, status : Draft

If your screen turns black after you sign in, stay calm. Your files and work data are safe. This usually affects what you can see on screen, not your saved documents.

## What you can do now

1. Wait 60 seconds.
Sometimes the desktop appears after a short delay.

2. If the screen is still black, sign out of your work desktop.
Use the menu in the sign-in app to sign out or close the connection.

3. Wait 60 seconds.
This gives it time to clear the problem.

4. Sign in again.
Check if your desktop opens normally.

5. If it still fails, restart your own laptop or PC.
After restart, try signing in one more time.

6. If the problem continues, contact the Service Desk.
Tell them you followed this help guide and the problem is still happening.

## Before you contact the Service Desk

Please share these details in your support request:

- Your full name and work login name
- The time the problem happened (include your local time)
- What you saw on screen (for example: black screen, error text)
- How long you waited before trying again
- Whether sign out and sign in helped
- Whether restarting your laptop or PC helped
- If possible, attach a screen photo of the black screen
- Best phone number or Teams contact so they can call you back

## Good to know

- Your saved work is still safe.
- Do not keep trying many times in a row; this can make the fix take longer.
- One or two careful tries are enough before asking for help.

Contact the Service Desk if the screen is still black after the steps above, or if you cannot work normally.
