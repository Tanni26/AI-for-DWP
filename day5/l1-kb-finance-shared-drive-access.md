# Finance Shared Drive Not Opening - Self Help

Version: 1.0 | Date: 07/08/2026 | Status: Draft

Your files are safe. If the Finance shared drive does not appear after you log in, try the steps below.

## What you can do safely
1. Save your work and close open apps.
2. Sign out of Windows.
3. Wait 60 seconds.
4. Sign in again.
5. Wait 2 to 3 minutes after sign-in.
6. Open File Explorer and check if drive S: appears.
7. If S: is still missing, restart your computer once.
8. Sign in again and wait 2 to 3 minutes.

## What not to do
- Do not change computer settings.
- Do not run command-line steps.
- Do not remove your work account.

## Contact the Service Desk if...
Contact the Service Desk if S: is still missing after one restart and a second sign-in.

## Details to include in your ticket
- Your full name
- Your work username
- Your computer name (example: DESKTOP-FB041)
- The exact time the issue happened
- Whether S: is missing or not opening
- Whether sign-out and restart were already tried
- Whether nearby Finance colleagues have the same issue

These details help support restore access faster.
