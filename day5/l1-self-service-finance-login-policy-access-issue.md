# Finance Access Problem - Help You Can Do Yourself

Version: 1.0 | Date: 07/08/2026 | Status: Draft

If you cannot open Finance shared files after you log in, your data is safe and this can usually be fixed quickly.

## What you can do now
1. Save any open work.
2. Log out of Windows.
3. Wait 60 seconds.
4. Log in again.
5. Wait 2 to 3 minutes after logging in before opening your work programs.
6. If you use Wi-Fi, make sure you are connected to your usual office network.
7. If possible, plug in a network cable and log in again.
8. Restart your computer once.
9. After restart, log in and wait 2 to 3 minutes.

## What not to do
- Do not change computer settings.
- Do not install software or type command-line instructions.
- Do not remove your work login from the computer.

## If the issue continues
Contact IT support if the problem is still present after one restart and one new login attempt.

## Details to include in your support request
Please include these details when you contact IT support:
- Your full name
- Your work login name
- Your computer name (for example: DESKTOP-FB041)
- Your office location or whether you are working from home
- The exact time the issue happened
- What you were trying to open when it failed
- A short description of what you saw on screen
- Whether log out and restart were already tried
- Whether another Finance colleague nearby has the same issue

A clear support request with these details helps IT support fix the issue faster.
