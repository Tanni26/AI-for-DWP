## Version Header

- Approval: Service Desk Manager

## Symptoms
- You cannot sign in.
- Sign-in works, but desktop is still loading after 5 minutes.

## Quick Self-Service (Floor 6)
1. Restart your device once.
2. Sign in again and wait up to 5 minutes.
3. If still stuck, take a screenshot.  
   [Screenshot Placeholder – Login error or long-loading screen]
4. Ask one nearby teammate if they have the same issue.

## When to Contact IT
- You cannot sign in after restart.
- Login is still over 5 minutes.
- More than one user is affected.

## What to Send IT
- Name, desk location, and device name.
- Time of issue, screenshot, and exact error text.

## Routing Information
- Service Desk routes to EUC.
